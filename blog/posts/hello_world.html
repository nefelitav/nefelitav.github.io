Hello, world

30 September 2026

This is my first post. I’m starting this blog as a way to motivate myself to study, research, and organize what I learn along the way.

I often find myself reading about different topics, going down rabbit holes, and collecting bits of information without necessarily putting them together. I’d like this blog to be a place where I can turn that scattered knowledge into something more structured and useful.

For now, I don’t have a specific plan for what I’ll write about. I’ll probably explore whatever I’m curious about, things I’m learning, questions I’m trying to answer, and ideas I want to understand better.

Hopefully, over time, this will become a useful record of what I’ve learned and a resource I can come back to when I need to think through something or make a decision.
